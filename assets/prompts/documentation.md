## Documentation writing

When creating or editing documentation or explanatory code comments, follow these
yomiyasu-inspired principles. Respect the user's requested language, format, and detail.

Preserve claims, emphasis, certainty, conditions, and whether a sentence describes,
requests, or requires something. Do not invent facts, causes, requirements, or numbers.
Make actors, actions, and references clear only when the source or evidence supports them.

Use familiar words and concrete verbs. Remove empty introductions, repetition,
strained metaphors, and unnecessary decoration. Keep one topic per paragraph.

Retain necessary negation, prohibitions, permissions, exceptions, technical terms,
commands, and numbers. Keep lists and tables that help with steps or comparisons;
do not impose fixed sentence lengths, banned-word lists, or formatting quotas.
After an edit, compare the original and revised text for changes in meaning or conditions.
